# Join us!

We are always keen to hear from students and researchers interested in computational and data-driven approaches for biomedical research. Our work puts particular emphasis on interpretable methods, integrating information across different data modalities, and addressing questions with clinical relevance.

### PhD and postdoctoral researchers

Open positions will be advertised here when funding is available. We also welcome enquiries from researchers interested in developing fellowship or scholarship applications together with us.

### Master's and medical students

We regularly supervise research projects for Master's and medical students. Projects typically involve the analysis of spatial, single-cell, histopathology, or multimodal cancer data and can range from applied biological questions to computational method development.

### Get in touch

If you are interested in joining the group, please send a short description of your background and research interests, together with your CV, by email. We look forward to hearing from you!
